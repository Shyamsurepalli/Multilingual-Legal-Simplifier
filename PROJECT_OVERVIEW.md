# ⚖️ LegalEase — AI-Powered Multilingual Legal Document Simplifier

LegalEase is an innovative AI-driven digital assistant designed to bridge the gap between complex legal jargon ("legalese") and the average citizen. By leveraging advanced artificial intelligence, LegalEase parses complex legal contracts, agreements, and policies, translating them into simplified, plain language and uncovering hidden risks. 

Whether it is a rental agreement, an employment contract, or a non-disclosure agreement (NDA), LegalEase gives users the power to understand what they are signing—without needing a lawyer.

---

## 🌟 The Core Problem We Solve
Legal documents are notorious for being long, dense, and written in archaic terminology. Most people sign contracts without reading or fully understanding them due to:
*   **High Legal Costs:** Hiring a lawyer to review standard contracts is often too expensive for everyday transactions.
*   **Language Barriers:** Migrants, students, or international workers frequently deal with legal documents in a foreign language.
*   **Intimidation:** Dense clauses and complex sentences can lead to signing away rights, accepting unfair penalties, or agreeing to extreme notice periods without knowing it.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Simple Plain-Language Summaries
LegalEase takes complex, multi-page legal documents and condenses them into a clear, 3-to-5 sentence summary. It highlights the overall scope, key obligations, and the general purpose of the contract in plain language that anyone can understand.

### 2. Multi-Format Support (PDF, DOC, Images)
The system is built to handle documents in multiple formats:
*   **PDFs & Word Documents:** Upload standard digital contracts directly.
*   **Scanned Images:** Take a photo or upload an image (JPG, JPEG, PNG) of a printed contract, and the system's vision-based AI will extract the text for analysis.

### 3. Multilingual Capabilities
You can upload a document written in any language and select your native tongue as the target language (supporting 20+ languages including Hindi, Spanish, French, Telugu, German, Japanese, and more). The AI automatically translates the entire simplified breakdown into your chosen language.

### 4. Smart Risk Assessment & Mitigations
The system acts as a protective shield by identifying unfavorable or risky clauses. Risks are grouped into three severity levels:
*   🔴 **High Severity:** Clauses containing severe liabilities, high penalties, or major loss of rights.
*   🟡 **Medium Severity:** Notable terms like long notice periods, automatic renewals, or restrictive covenants.
*   🟢 **Low Severity:** Minor points of caution that are standard but worth noting.
For every identified risk, LegalEase provides a **plain-language explanation** of why it is risky and a **mitigation solution** (e.g., a counter-proposal to negotiate with the other party).

### 5. Key Terms Extraction
Instead of reading through pages of text to find simple numbers, the system extracts critical facts into a quick-reference list, including:
*   Financial figures (rent amounts, deposit figures, interest rates, penalties).
*   Important timeframes (notice periods, contract duration, grace periods).
*   Key entities and dates.

### 6. Interactive Legal Q&A Chat (RAG-powered)
Have a specific question? Users can chat interactively with their uploaded document. You can ask queries like:
*   *"Is there a penalty if I break this lease early?"*
*   *"What is the notice period required to resign?"*
*   *"Who is responsible for repairs in this agreement?"*
The AI queries the document directly and provides accurate, page-specific answers in the user's preferred language.

### 7. Private and Secure Digital Vault
Privacy is paramount when dealing with legal documents. LegalEase utilizes advanced symmetric encryption to protect all files and extracted text blocks. Only the authorized owner can view, download, or query their uploaded documents.
